# Mapparaan transit data

Static, schedule-based transit data **for the City of Manila only**. The routing
backend (`backend/planner`) reads `gtfs/` and `reference/`; the Flutter app talks
to that backend, not to these files directly.

## Scope: City of Manila

`raw/` keeps the full Metro Manila feed. `gtfs/` is clipped to the official City
of Manila boundary (`reference/manila_boundary.geojson`, PSGC 1380600000,
from faeldon/philippines-json-maps, 2023, MIT):

- a stop is kept if it is inside the boundary (real polygon, not a box) **or within
  50 m of its edge** (`--tolerance-m`, default 50). Source coordinates are rounded to
  about 3 decimals (up to ~55 m error), so a stop on the city line can land just
  outside the polygon: a strict test wrongly dropped "Bonifacio Dr, Manila",
  "Halcon / Florentino Intersection, Manila" and "Roxas Blvd / Pablo Ocampo Sr.,
  Manila", and split a North Harbor trip in two. A stop in that 50 m band is skipped
  if its name names another city (e.g. "Quezon City"). Names are never used to add a
  stop or to drop one inside the polygon. `--tolerance-m 0` gives a strict test.
  Stops 50-250 m outside are listed in `tools`' `--review-csv` output for manual review
- each trip is cut into runs of consecutive inside-stops; a trip that leaves and
  re-enters becomes two trips (`_a`, `_b`); runs under 2 stops are dropped
- shapes are cut to the inside part
- MRT-3 does not enter the City of Manila, so it is not in the feed
- some LTFRB stop names say Quezon City / Caloocan: those are LTFRB's labels,
  the coordinates are inside the boundary (border stops such as LRT Roosevelt-side
  stations)

Result: 543 stops, 600 routes (561 jeepney, 36 bus, LRT-1, LRT-2, PNR), 671 trips.
    
## Layout

```
data/
├── README.md                 this file
├── raw/
│   └── sakay_gtfs/           UNTOUCHED copy of the source feed (incl. LICENSE.md)
├── gtfs/                     patched, standard GTFS files only
│   ├── agency.txt            3 agencies (LRTA, LTFRB, PNR)
│   ├── routes.txt            600 routes
│   ├── trips.txt             671 trips
│   ├── stops.txt             543 stops
│   ├── stop_times.txt        11,256 rows (times relative to trip start)
│   ├── frequencies.txt       headway-based service (no fixed timetables)
│   ├── calendar.txt          8 service patterns
│   ├── shapes.txt            7 shapes (rail + 1 route), clipped
│   └── feed_info.txt
├── reference/                Mapparaan's own data (NOT part of GTFS)
│   ├── manila_boundary.geojson  City of Manila polygon used for clipping
│   ├── route_modes.csv       route_id -> mode
│   ├── fare_config.json      fare rules + discounts
│   └── manifest.json         row counts and build info
└── build/
    └── mapparaan_gtfs.zip    what OpenTripPlanner loads
```

Rule of thumb: **raw/** is never edited, **gtfs/** and **reference/** and
**build/** are generated by `tools/patch_gtfs.py` and can be deleted and rebuilt
at any time. Hand edits belong in the script, not in the generated files.

## Rebuild

```
# 1. patch the full feed (calendar dates + route_short_name, see "Patches" below),
#    then clip it to Manila:
python3 tools/clip_gtfs.py <patched_full_feed_dir> data/reference/manila_boundary.geojson data/gtfs
# options:  --tolerance-m 50 (default; 0 = strict polygon)   --review-csv stops_to_review.csv
```

`clip_gtfs.py` expects the already-patched full Metro Manila feed as input. With
`--tolerance-m 0` it reproduces the previous 540-stop / 672-trip feed exactly.

## Schemas

**reference/route_modes.csv**

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| route_id | matches `routes.txt` |
| mode | `jeepney`, `bus`, `lrt1`, `lrt2`, `mrt3`, `pnr`, `fort_bus` (`uv_express` reserved, none in data yet) |

Mode comes from LTFRB's route codes: `PUJ` = jeepney (561 here), `PUB` = bus (36 here).

**reference/fare_config.json**

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `discount.multiplier` | 0.8 (20% off), non-stackable, student / senior / PWD |
| `modes.<mode>.base_fare`, `base_km`, `per_km` | distance-based fare: base covers `base_km`, then `per_km` after |
| `modes.<mode>.verified` | `false` until checked against the current LTFRB / DOTr fare order |
| rail modes | `table: null` until the distance-based fare matrices are added |

Fare = `base_fare` + `per_km` × (distance_km − `base_km`) beyond the base
distance, then × `discount.multiplier` if eligible. **All current values are
placeholders.**

## Patches applied to the source feed

1. `calendar.txt`: start/end dates set to 2020-01-01 – 2035-12-31 (source expired 2020-06-30). Dates are stretched only; the schedules themselves are still the original data.
2. `routes.txt`: empty `route_short_name` filled from the LTFRB code (e.g. `PUJ1094`). `route_type` untouched.

## Known gaps

- Data is circa 2013–15 (DOTC / Philippine Transit App Challenge); some routes will be outdated or missing.
- No road geometry for jeepney/bus routes: they draw as straight lines between stops until shapes are generated.
- No UV Express routes.
- No fares in the GTFS; fares come from `fare_config.json`.
- Schedules are headway-based and approximate (not live, not vehicle-specific).
